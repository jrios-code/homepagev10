Context Engine

One intelligence layer across every system you run.

Most companies are adopting AI one tool at a time: a pilot here, a plug-in there, each one added on top of how work already gets done. The results stay shallow and scattered. AI ends up as a feature instead of a foundation.

The Context Engine provides you with a single intelligence layer that runs across your whole business, fed by your existing systems and data, built on your definitions and KPIs. A platform you can engage with in real time and put agents to work across every function.

  1. Data & Intelligence

    Connects every system, from CRM and ERP to phones and email, into one live view. Every number links back to its source.

    • Weekly reporting that builds itself
    • Margin by job or client
    • Attribution and CAC
    • Call intelligence

  2. Inference on Your Data

    Finds patterns and predicts what's coming, using your own definitions. What your people know stays after they leave.

    • Churn prediction
    • Capacity and demand forecasting
    • Signed-but-inactive alerts
    • Lead triage

  3. Agents that Act

    Drafts the next step and stages it in your own tools, on its own or with your approval, across sales, marketing, ops, finance, HR and support.

    • Follow-ups staged for approval
    • Field calls and texts into the CRM

    Industry agents in development: invoice audit, revenue recapture, collections, medical records.

    What leadership teams ask us first

    What does RubeTech cost?

    Most work starts with a Discovery Assessment, from $25,000 over about six weeks. It's priced on its own, and any engagement after it is scoped and priced separately. The pricing section shows starting points for the 100-Day Plan, CRM Rescue, the Revenue Engine Build and the Context Engine, which starts at $30,000 a month. Final pricing moves with company size, the number of locations and systems, and scope.

    How do we use AI to make our people more efficient without cutting jobs?

    Start with the manual work that fills their week: re-keying, reporting and chasing follow-ups. We automate it and put agents on it, so your sales, marketing and operations teams spend their time on higher-value work. Most clients use the gain to grow without adding headcount.

    What happens on the first call?

    Forty-five minutes with a senior operator. You bring the problem or decision in front of you, and we ask how your company runs around it. You leave with our view. If working together makes sense, we'll outline a Discovery Assessment, including its length, its cost and who on your team it involves. If it doesn't, we'll say so.

    How is the Context Engine different from giving our team ChatGPT or Claude?

    A chat assistant knows what one person pastes into it. The Context Engine is connected to all of your systems, uses your own definitions and KPIs, keeps what your people know after they leave, and stages the next step in your own tools. It runs on about 15 models, so it isn't tied to any one of them.

    We already have an IT lead. Where do you fit?

    Next to them. Your IT lead keeps your systems running and stays in charge of them. We add the capacity they rarely have time for: mapping how work moves between systems, planning what to connect or retire, and engineering the builds.

    I'm the COO. How do I bring this to my CEO?

    Send them this page, or book the conversation together so you both hear the same answers. Most of the questions we start with are ones the CEO will ask anyway: what the problem costs the business today, what changes first, and how much of the team's time it takes.

    How long does the Discovery Assessment take, and how much of our team's time?

    Usually about six weeks. Most people spend about 45 minutes with us once, and the people closest to the problem spend more. We schedule around your busy periods.

    Do we have to replace our systems?

    No. Most of our work starts with what you already run. When something does need to go, you'll know why before anything changes.

    What size company do you work with?

    Larger SMB and mid-market companies, owner-led or PE-backed, with roughly $25M to $300M in revenue and often several locations. Below that range the economics rarely work for either of us, and we'll tell you.

    Do you work with private equity–backed companies?

    Yes. We work with PE-backed operating companies, and with sponsors who want the same approach across several portfolio companies running on different systems.

    Do we own what you build, and where does our data live?

    Your data stays yours, and custom builds are yours. The Context Engine is licensed. It runs in your cloud, on your own servers or fully in a closed loop, and where data does leave your environment, sensitive fields are hashed first.

    Are you a HubSpot agency?

    No. We're a consultancy with a revenue operations practice and a certified HubSpot Partner. For a revenue operation, we implement HubSpot or Salesforce and deploy HubSpot's AI agents. For intelligence and agents across every department, that's the Context Engine. We'll tell you when the problem is bigger than the CRM.
